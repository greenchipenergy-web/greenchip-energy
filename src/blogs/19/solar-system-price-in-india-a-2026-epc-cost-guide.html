PM Surya Ghar Subsidy — Kitni Milegi Aur Kaise?

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (PMSG:MBY) is the single biggest reason rooftop solar adoption has exploded in India. Launched on 13 February 2024, the scheme provides Central Financial Assistance (CFA) directly to homeowners' bank accounts via DBT. Here's the subsidy structure as per the MNRE guidelines (revised July 2025):

System SizeCFA RateTotal Central Subsidy
First 2 kW₹30,000/kW₹60,000
Additional 1 kW (up to 3kW)₹18,000/kW₹18,000
Maximum (3kW cap)—₹78,000

The central subsidy is capped at 3kW. If you install a 5kW or 10kW system, the CFA stays at ₹78,000. It applies only to on-grid residential systems installed by DISCOM-empanelled installers using ALMM-listed panels. Off-grid and hybrid systems are not eligible. Several states offer additional top-ups — Gujarat's SURYA scheme, Maharashtra's residential incentive, UPNEDA's supplementary subsidy for UP residents, and Delhi's Generation-Based Incentive (GBI). One critical compliance requirement: panels must appear on MNRE's Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM). Non-ALMM panels lead to guaranteed subsidy rejection. For accurate eligibility assessment, consult with an experienced GreenChip Energy or similar EPC provider who understands the documentation and DISCOM process.

Solar Quote Ka Poora Breakdown — Hidden Charges Jo Koi Nahi Batata

When you receive a solar installation quote, the headline number rarely tells the full story. Most quotes list "₹X per kW" but leave out several line items that can add ₹10,000–₹30,000 to your final bill. A transparent EPC quote should include: solar panels (with brand, wattage, and ALMM listing), grid-tie inverter (brand and warranty), mounting structure (GI or aluminium with earthing), DC/AC cabling and junction boxes, net-metering application, DISCOM liaison, installation labour, and commissioning.

What often gets missed: earthing and lightning arrestor (₹3,000–₹8,000), waterproofing for roof penetrations (₹2,000–₹5,000), net-metering security deposit (₹1,000–₹5,000, varies by DISCOM), surge protection device (₹1,500–₹3,000), roof strengthening for old structures (₹5,000–₹20,000), and annual maintenance contracts (₹2,000–₹8,000/year). The best way to verify your quote is to request a line-item breakdown instead of accepting a lump-sum number. A professional EPC provider — whether it's Smart Solar EPC Solutions in India or another qualified installer — should walk you through every component before you sign. Also verify that the installer is DISCOM-empanelled; only empanelled installers can process the PM Surya Ghar subsidy on your behalf.

On-Grid vs Hybrid vs Off-Grid — Aapke Ghar Ke Liye Kaunsa Sahi?

Choosing the right system type is as important as choosing the right size. Each configuration serves a different need, and the solar system price in India varies significantly across them. An on-grid (grid-tied) system is the most popular and cost-effective option for urban homes — surplus electricity goes to the grid via net-metering, earning credits on your bill, with no batteries needed. The PM Surya Ghar subsidy applies only to this type.

An off-grid system is completely independent of the grid with battery storage for nighttime use — ideal for remote areas, but batteries add 30–50% to costs and need replacement every 5–7 years, with no subsidy available. A hybrid system combines both: grid connection with battery backup, costing 20–40% more than pure on-grid setups.

Installer Kaise Chunein — Red Flags Aur Expert Tips

Choosing the right installer is arguably more important than choosing the right panels. A poorly installed system — even with top-tier components — can underperform, void warranties, or lead to subsidy rejection. Key red flags: no DISCOM/MNRE empanelment (they cannot process your PM Surya Ghar subsidy), no ALMM-listed panels (guaranteed subsidy rejection), vague quotes without line-item breakdowns, no O&M offering, and high-pressure "sign today" tactics.

Expert tips for 2026: get at least 2–3 quotes and compare line by line, not just the bottom line. Ask for ALMM certificate copies and DISCOM empanelment proof before signing. Verify that the inverter brand has a service centre in your city for warranty claims. Discuss the net-metering timeline upfront — delays in meter installation mean delays in subsidy disbursement. FAQs

1. What is the solar system price in India in 2026?

The solar system price in India in 2026 typically ranges from ₹55,000–₹70,000 per kW before subsidy for residential rooftop systems. After the PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000), a 3kW system can cost ₹85,000–₹1,17,000 out of pocket.

2. How much subsidy does PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana provide?

The scheme provides ₹30,000/kW for the first 2kW and ₹18,000/kW for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000 per household. It applies only to on-grid systems installed by DISCOM-empanelled installers using ALMM-listed panels.

3. Is solar subsidy available for 5kW or 10kW systems?

Yes, but the central subsidy remains capped at ₹78,000 regardless of system size above 3kW. Some states offer additional top-ups that may apply to larger systems.

4. How long does it take to recover the solar investment?

For 1–3kW systems, payback typically occurs in 3–4 years. For 5kW+ systems, payback may take 4–6 years depending on your DISCOM tariff rate and local sunlight conditions.

5. Can I get a solar loan for rooftop installation?

Yes. SBI, Canara Bank, HDFC, and Bajaj Finserv offer solar loans at 7–11% interest with tenures up to 10 years. In many cases, the monthly EMI is comparable to the electricity bill it replaces.

6. What happens if my installer is not DISCOM-empanelled?

Your PM Surya Ghar subsidy application will be rejected. Only MNRE/DISCOM-empanelled installers are authorised to process the subsidy. Always verify empanelment status before signing a contract.

7. Are hybrid or off-grid systems eligible for subsidy?

No. The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana provides subsidy only for on-grid (grid-tied) residential rooftop solar systems. Off-grid and hybrid battery systems are not eligible.
