What Is a Solar EPC Company and How Does It Work in India

A solar EPC company manages the complete lifecycle of a solar power plant under a single turnkey contract. EPC stands for Engineering, Procurement, and Construction — three phases bundled into one delivery model. Engineering covers system design, shading analysis, and electrical layout. Procurement involves sourcing solar modules, inverters, mounting structures, and balance-of-system components from verified manufacturers. Construction brings everything on-site through panel installation, wiring, and grid synchronization through to commissioning and performance validation.

In India's context, a solar EPC contractor like GreenChip Energy handles this full scope across residential rooftops, commercial and industrial systems, and utility-scale ground-mount plants. The core value of the EPC model is single-provider accountability — one entity owns design quality, component sourcing, installation standards, and commissioning outcomes. Many EPCs also offer Operations and Maintenance (O&M) as an add-on for long-term plant performance. Understanding this turnkey scope is the starting point for evaluating proposals when comparing the best solar EPC company India offers.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy, Net-Metering & MNRE Empanelment Checklist

For residential and small-commercial buyers, MNRE empanelment and PM Surya Ghar subsidy processing capability are critical EPC selection criteria. Verify these five items before signing:

  1. MNRE/DISCOM Empanelment: Confirm the EPC is listed on your state DISCOM's approved vendor roster — empanelment is state-specific, not national.
  2. ALMM-Listed Modules: Only modules on MNRE's Approved List of Models and Manufacturers qualify for government subsidies and most C&I projects.
  3. PM Surya Ghar Portal Registration: Your EPC must be registered on the national portal to process subsidy claims. With 16,328 systems installed daily in July 2026, choose EPCs with dedicated subsidy processing teams.
  4. Net-Metering Application Handling: The best EPCs manage the entire DISCOM application, inspection scheduling, and meter installation process on your behalf.
  5. Subsidy Disbursement Tracking: Top EPCs provide visibility into your subsidy status post-installation — critical given the ₹27,344 crore disbursed to date.

Red Flags & Risk Checklist Before Signing an EPC Contract

Before signing any solar EPC contract, watch for these warning signs:

  • No reference projects: Legitimate EPCs share completed project references willingly. Refusal to arrange site visits is a major red flag.
  • Vague performance guarantees: Demand specific P50/P90 energy yield commitments in writing — not just "the system will perform well."
  • Non-ALMM modules: If the EPC proposes modules not on MNRE's ALMM list, you risk subsidy disqualification and potential quality issues.
  • Missing performance bonds: For projects above 100 kW, request a Performance Bank Guarantee or LC covering system performance for 1–2 years post-commissioning.
  • No O&M plan: A credible EPC should propose at least a 5-year O&M plan with defined response times and performance monitoring.
  • Unrealistic timelines: Be cautious of promises at half the industry-standard duration — quality shortcuts often follow.

Always verify the EPC's financial health, request written guarantees, and compare at least three proposals before committing to any provider.
