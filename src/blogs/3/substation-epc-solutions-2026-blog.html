HVDC Converter Substations: A Specialized Capability

For high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission systems, expertise in converter substations shines. We create specialized facilities that facilitate the efficient conversion of alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC) or vice versa for long-distance power transmission, incorporating advanced equipment such as converters, transformers, filters, and control systems. HVDC conversion is a genuinely specialized technical capability, distinct from standard AC substation work, relevant specifically for long-distance power transmission projects.
