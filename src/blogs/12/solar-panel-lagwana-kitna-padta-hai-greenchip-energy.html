1. Solar system ka kharcha kin cheezon par depend karta hai

Log aksar sirf "per kW kitna" puchte hain. Lekin do quotes tabhi compare ho sakte hain jab unme ye sab cheezein same ho:

  • System size (kW): Jitna bada system, utna total kharcha, lekin per kW rate aam taur par thoda kam hota jaata hai.
  • Solar panels: Mono PERC ya TOPCon jaise modules, brand, warranty (product aur performance dono) aur efficiency. Sasta panel shuru me bachat lagta hai, lekin 25 saal ki generation me fark dikhta hai.
  • Inverter: String inverter ya hybrid inverter. Agar battery chahiye (off-grid ya hybrid), to kharcha badh jaata hai.
  • Mounting structure: Galvanised iron structure, roof ki height, shed ya RCC roof - sab cost par asar daalte hain.
  • Wiring, earthing, protection: DC/AC cables, lightning arrester, earthing aur MCB/SPD. Ye hissa kam na karein, ye safety ka mamla hai.
  • Net meter aur discom charges: Bijli company ke fees aur meter ka kharcha.
  • Installation aur transport: Site ki location aur roof tak pahunch ka asar.
  • Monitoring system: App ya portal jo roz ki generation dikhata hai.

Indicative idea: India me rooftop solar ka market cost aam taur par kuch ₹ per watt ke range me hota hai, aur ye saal dar saal badalta rehta hai. Isliye is blog me hum koi ek fixed price nahi likh rahe. Aapka sahi cost aapki roof, load aur choice ke hisab se GreenChip Energy ke quote me hi milega.

2. Ghar ke liye kaun sa size chahiye?

Thumb rule ye hai ki 1 kW rooftop solar system se roz lagbhag 4 se 5 units bijli ban sakti hai (location, season aur shading par depend karta hai). Isse mahine ke lagbhag 120 se 150 units ban jaate hain. PM Surya Ghar portal ke hisab se average monthly consumption ke mutabik suitable capacity ye hai:

Average monthly bijli use (units)Suitable rooftop solar capacityCentral subsidy (scheme ke hisab se)
0 - 1501 - 2 kW₹30,000 se ₹60,000
150 - 3002 - 3 kW₹60,000 se ₹78,000
300 se upar3 kW se zyada₹78,000 (cap)

Source: PIB - PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.

Apna size tay karne ke liye last 12 mahine ke bills nikaalo, average units nikaalo, aur phir roof par available shadow-free jagah check karo. 1 kW ke liye lagbhag 80 to 100 square feet shadow-free jagah chahiye hoti hai. Ye number panel ke type ke saath thoda badalta hai.

3. Subsidy kitni milti hai? (PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana)

PM Surya Ghar scheme ka maqsad residential households ko rooftop solar ke saath bijli bill kam karne me madad dena hai. Scheme ke official portal aur sarkari documents ke mutabik central financial assistance (CFA) is tarah hai:

  • 2 kW tak: ₹30,000 per kW
  • 3rd kW ke liye: ₹18,000 per kW
  • 3 kW ya usse bade residential system par subsidy ₹78,000 par capped hai

Detail aur latest rules ke liye National Portal for Rooftop Solar aur MyScheme par scheme page dekhein. Subsidy ke rules badal sakte hain, isliye apply karne se pehle current guidelines zaroor check karein.

Zaroori baat: Ye subsidy residential households ke liye hai. Commercial aur industrial projects ke liye alag incentives (jaise accelerated depreciation ya state ki policy) ho sakti hain, jo aapke CA ya tax advisor se confirm karni chahiye.

4. Net metering kya hai aur Telangana me kaise hoti hai?

Net metering me aapka rooftop solar system din me bijli banata hai. Jo bijli aap use nahi karte, wo grid me chali jaati hai, aur bill me adjust ho jaati hai. Raat ko aap grid se bijli lete ho. Mahine ke end me sirf "net" units ka bill aata hai.

Telangana me TGSPDCL ki Solar FAQs ke mutabik net metering preferably 3-phase consumers ko di jaati hai, lekin single phase consumers bhi 5 kW tak ke rooftop system par eligible hain. Residential aur government consumers ke liye maximum rooftop capacity sanctioned load ka 100% aur industrial, commercial aur others ke liye 80% hai. Online registration ke liye TGSPDCL Net Meter portal use hota hai. Latest regulation ke liye Net Metering Regulation 01 of 2025 dekh sakte hain.

Agar aap Telangana ke dusre discom area me ho ya kisi aur state me, to wahan ke discom ki process alag ho sakti hai. Vendor ko pehle hi pooch lo ki wo net metering ka poora paperwork sambhalta hai ya nahi.

6. Step-by-step: solar lagwane ki process

  1. Bill check karo: Last 12 mahine ke bills aur sanctioned load dekho.
  2. Roof ya jagah check karo: Shadow-free area, roof ki strength, direction (south-facing best mani jaati hai).
  3. Site survey karwao: Vendor ki team aakar roof, load aur shading dekhti hai.
  4. Quote aur design lo: Quote me kW size, panel brand, inverter, structure, warranty, net meter aur subsidy paperwork clearly likha ho.
  5. Subsidy ke liye apply karo (agar eligible ho): National Portal par registration, discom aur vendor selection. Portal ke hisab se jab credentials sahi ho to CFA processing me lagbhag 15 din lagte hain redemption request ke baad.
  6. Installation: Aam ghar ke system me kuch din lagte hain; bade commercial projects me zyada time lagta hai.
  7. Net meter aur inspection: Discom meter lagata hai aur system ko approve karta hai.
  8. Commissioning aur monitoring: System start hota hai, aur app ya portal par generation dikhne lagti hai.
  9. Maintenance: Saal me kuch baar panel safai aur health check.

7. Payback kitne saal me aata hai?

Payback ka matlab hai ki bill ki bachat se aapka invest kiya hua paisa kitne saal me wapas aa jaata hai. Ye teen cheezon par depend karta hai: aapka tariff (jitna zyada bijli rate, utna jaldi payback), system ka size aur use pattern, aur subsidy ya tax benefit. Jo business din me zyada bijli use karte hain, unka solar generation aur consumption match hota hai, isliye unka payback aksar tez hota hai.

Isko aise nikaalo: (system ki total cost - subsidy) / (saal bhar ki bijli bill bachat). GreenChip Energy ki website par likha hai ki solar se bijli bill me bahut badi kami laayi ja sakti hai (home page par "up to 70%" ka zikr hai), lekin aapki asli bachat aapke load aur system design par depend karegi, isliye quote ke saath expected generation report maangein.

FAQ: Solar Panel Lagwana Kitna Padta Hai

Q1. Ghar me solar panel lagwane me kitna kharcha aata hai?

Kharcha system ke size (kW), panel aur inverter ki quality, roof ke type aur installation par depend karta hai. 2 kW se 3 kW ka system aam tor par chhote aur medium ghar ke liye hota hai. Sahi kharcha site survey ke baad quote se hi pata chalta hai. Subsidy milne par aapki net kharcha kam ho jaati hai.

Q2. 3 kW solar system par kitni subsidy milti hai?

PM Surya Ghar scheme ke mutabik 2 kW tak ₹30,000 per kW aur 3rd kW ke liye ₹18,000 milte hain, yaani 3 kW par lagbhag ₹78,000 tak. 3 kW se bade residential system par bhi subsidy ₹78,000 par capped hai. Latest rules ke liye pmsuryaghar.gov.in dekhein.

Q3. Net metering kya hai?

Net metering me aapka solar system din me jo extra bijli banata hai wo grid me chali jaati hai aur bill me adjust hoti hai. Mahine ke end me sirf net units ka bill aata hai.

Q4. Solar system ka payback kitne saal me aata hai?

Payback aapke bijli rate, use pattern, system cost aur subsidy par depend karta hai. Iska formula hai: (total cost - subsidy) / saalana bill bachat. Quote ke saath expected generation report maangein.

Q5. Kya single phase connection par solar lag sakta hai?

Telangana me TGSPDCL ke mutabik single phase consumers bhi 5 kW tak ke rooftop system par net metering ke liye eligible hain. Aapke state ke rules alag ho sakte hain, isliye apne discom se confirm karein.

Q6. Solar panel kitne saal chalte hain aur maintenance kya hai?

Achhe quality ke panels par aam taur par lambi performance warranty (aksar 25 saal ke aaspaas) milti hai. Maintenance me mukhya kaam panel ki safai aur system ka periodic check hota hai. Exact warranty quote me likhi honi chahiye.
