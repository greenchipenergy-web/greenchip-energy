PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 Subsidy: How Hyderabad Homes Claim It

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana offers central government subsidies for residential rooftop solar installations in Telangana. For systems up to 3 kW, the subsidy amounts to ₹78,000 (₹30,000 for the first kW, ₹30,000 for the second kW, and ₹18,000 for the third kW). For systems between 3–10 kW, the subsidy is ₹78,000 for the first 3 kW plus ₹18,000 for each additional kW, capped at the applicable ceiling.

To claim the subsidy in Hyderabad, register on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar (solarrooftop.gov.in). Select your DISCOM — TSSPDCL for most Hyderabad addresses — and choose a vendor from the approved list. Submit your application with required documents: electricity bill, property proof, Aadhaar, and bank details. After TSSPDCL technical feasibility approval, your installer proceeds with installation. Once commissioned, the DISCOM installs a net meter and issues a commissioning certificate. The subsidy is then credited directly to your bank account.

Eligibility criteria are straightforward: the applicant must be a residential consumer with a valid TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL connection, the system must use ALMM-listed (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) panels, and the installation must be handled by a portal-listed vendor. Common subsidy rejection reasons include mismatched consumer names between the electricity bill and application, non-ALMM panel usage, and incomplete documentation. Working with an experienced solar EPC company that manages subsidy paperwork alongside installation can reduce processing delays and ensure your application is submitted correctly the first time.

TSSPDCL Net-Metering Process & Approval Timeline

Net metering allows your rooftop solar system to export surplus electricity to the grid, earning credits on your TSSPDCL electricity bill. In Hyderabad, the process follows a standardized sequence — but timelines can stretch if documentation is incomplete.

Step-by-step TSSPDCL net-metering process:

  1. Apply online via TSSPDCL's portal or the PM Surya Ghar national portal with your consumer number, electricity bill, and proposed system details.
  2. Technical feasibility check — TSSPDCL inspects your meter, transformer capacity, and grid proximity. Approval typically takes 15–30 days.
  3. Install the system — your empanelled solar EPC company completes installation and submits a commissioning report to TSSPDCL.
  4. Net meter installation — TSSPDCL replaces your existing meter with a bidirectional net meter. This step can take 7–15 days after commissioning.
  5. Synchronization and billing — once the net meter is active, surplus units are credited in your billing cycle.

The entire process, from application to active net metering, typically takes 45–90 days in Hyderabad. Delays commonly occur during the technical feasibility stage if the local transformer is at capacity or during net meter procurement. Your solar EPC company should handle the full TSSPDCL application on your behalf, including document submission, follow-ups, and coordination for net meter installation. This is a key differentiator when selecting an installer — companies that manage the DISCOM process end-to-end save you weeks of bureaucratic back-and-forth. For commercial and industrial facilities, the process is similar but may require additional approvals for higher-capacity systems.

Red Flags to Avoid When Hiring a Hyderabad Solar Company

The rapid growth of solar adoption in Hyderabad has attracted both established EPC firms and opportunistic operators. Watch for these warning signs before signing any contract.

100% advance payment requests. Legitimate solar EPC companies structure payments in milestones — typically a booking amount, a mid-installation payment, and a final payment after commissioning. If a company demands full payment upfront, walk away. Non-ALMM panels. The Approved List of Models and Manufacturers is a government-mandated quality standard. Using non-ALMM panels disqualifies your system from the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and may void DISCOM approval for net metering. Always verify the panel model number against the ALMM list.

No GST invoice. A GST-compliant invoice is essential for subsidy claims, warranty registration, and tax benefits (accelerated depreciation for commercial systems). Vague warranty terms. "25-year warranty" means nothing without specifics — confirm whether it covers panel performance degradation (typically guaranteed at 80–90% output at year 25), product defects, and workmanship separately. No O&M offering. Solar panels need periodic cleaning, inverter checks, and performance monitoring. Companies that disappear after installation leave you with a system that degrades faster than expected. Unrealistic savings promises. Be cautious of installers guaranteeing specific monthly savings or exact payback timelines — actual returns depend on your consumption pattern, tariff slab, shadow profile, and system performance, all of which vary case by case.

Hyderabad Solar FAQs: Cost, Subsidy, Payback & Warranty

How much does a 3 kW solar system cost in Hyderabad after subsidy?

A 3 kW rooftop solar system typically costs ₹1.8–2.6 lakh before subsidy. After the PM Surya Ghar central subsidy of ₹78,000, the net cost ranges from approximately ₹1.02–1.82 lakh, depending on panel brand, inverter make, and roof type.

Is Hyderabad good for solar energy?

Yes. Hyderabad receives approximately 5.5–6.0 kWh/m²/day of solar irradiance and enjoys 300+ sunny days annually. TSSPDCL tariff slabs of ₹6.05–₹9.95/unit create strong financial returns for rooftop solar.

How long does TSSPDCL net metering approval take?

From application to active net metering, the process typically takes 45–90 days in Hyderabad. The timeline depends on local transformer capacity and documentation completeness.

Can apartment complexes install rooftop solar in Hyderabad?

Yes. Common areas of apartment complexes can host solar systems, and individual flat owners may apply separately for designated terrace areas. Subsidy eligibility and the net-metering process remain the same, though society-level coordination is needed.

What warranty should I expect from a Hyderabad solar company?

Standard warranty includes a 25-year panel performance warranty (degradation guarantee), 10–12 year product warranty on panels, 5–10 year warranty on inverters, and 1–5 year workmanship warranty. Specific terms vary by manufacturer and installer.
