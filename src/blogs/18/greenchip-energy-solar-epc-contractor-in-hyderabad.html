PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 Subsidy: How Hyderabad Homes Claim It

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana has reshaped residential rooftop solar economics across India. According to the Press Information Bureau, the scheme crossed 10.09 lakh rooftop installations by March 2025, disbursing ₹4,770 crore in subsidies to over 6.13 lakh beneficiaries. For Hyderabad homeowners, the central subsidy offers ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for additional capacity up to 3 kW — bringing the maximum benefit to ₹78,000. The scheme mandates Indian-made modules listed under the ALMM and provides collateral-free loans at 6.75% interest through twelve public-sector banks.

To claim the subsidy, homeowners register on the national PM Surya Ghar portal, select a TGREDCO- or DISCOM-empanelled installer, and complete the installation with net-metering approval. The subsidy is then credited to the homeowner's bank account — typically within 30–60 days, though timelines vary by division. One frequent rejection reason is the use of non-ALMM panels or incomplete TSSPDCL connectivity paperwork. If you are exploring smart solar EPC solutions in India, confirm that your contractor is fluent in the PM Surya Ghar application workflow, ALMM compliance, and DISCOM documentation. A capable EPC partner prevents these errors proactively — and in a market where the subsidy alone can reduce a 3 kW system's net cost by 30–40%, getting the paperwork right the first time matters.

TSSPDCL Net-Metering Process & Approval Timeline

Net metering lets Hyderabad homeowners export surplus solar electricity to the TSSPDCL grid and receive credits on their monthly bill — effectively spinning the meter backwards during peak sunlight hours. The process starts after your EPC contractor completes installation and commissioning. You or your contractor submits a net-metering application to the local TSSPDCL division office, accompanied by the system's electrical single-line diagram, equipment datasheets, and an installer test certificate. TSSPDCL then schedules a site inspection, installs a bidirectional meter, and issues the connectivity agreement.

Realistic timelines range from two to eight weeks, depending on division workload and documentation quality. Common delays include mismatched system capacity on the application, missing ALMM compliance certificates, or structural clearance gaps for apartment buildings. Working with an experienced GreenChip Energy EPC services provider — or a similarly capable contractor who manages the entire TSSPDCL workflow from application filing to meter coordination — significantly reduces rejection risk and waiting periods. Some contractors also assist with post-installation DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) verification needed for PM Surya Ghar subsidy disbursement. Before signing any contract, confirm in writing that net-metering application filing, follow-up, and connectivity agreement collection are included in your EPC scope rather than billed as an optional add-on.

How to Choose the Best Solar Installer in Hyderabad: 12-Point Checklist

A rooftop solar system is a 25-year asset — the installer you choose determines whether those decades deliver reliable savings or recurring headaches. Use this 12-point checklist before signing any contract in Hyderabad:

  1. ALMM-listed panels — Confirm proposed modules appear on MNRE's Approved Models and Manufacturers list.
  2. Itemised quotation — Insist on a breakup: panel cost, inverter cost, mounting structure, wiring, labour, and GST listed separately.
  3. Workmanship warranty — Look for a minimum 5-year installation warranty alongside the 25-year panel performance warranty.
  4. TSSPDCL net-metering handling — Verify the contractor files and follows up on your net-metering application.
  5. PM Surya Ghar subsidy assistance — The contractor should guide you through portal registration and claim documentation.
  6. Site survey before quoting — Avoid companies that quote without a physical site visit; shadow and structural analysis are essential.
  7. Inverter brand and type — Ask whether string or micro-inverters suit your specific roof orientation.
  8. GST invoice — A compliant contractor provides a full GST invoice, critical for subsidy claims and warranty validation.
  9. Post-installation support — Clarify response-time commitments for fault rectification within the first year.
  10. Hyderabad references — Request contact details from at least two completed projects in your locality.
  11. Advance payment terms — Reasonable deposits range from 10–30%; avoid contractors demanding 100% upfront.
  12. Written scope of work — The agreement should list every deliverable, timeline, and warranty term explicitly.

Hyderabad Solar FAQs: Cost, Subsidy, Payback & Warranty

What is a solar EPC contractor in Hyderabad?

A solar EPC contractor in Hyderabad handles the complete Engineering, Procurement, and Construction lifecycle of your rooftop or ground-mount solar project — from system design and component sourcing to installation, commissioning, and grid synchronisation with TSSPDCL. This turnkey model means one provider is accountable for the entire delivery.

How much does a 3 kW rooftop solar system cost in Hyderabad after subsidy?

In 2026, a 3 kW system typically costs ₹1.8–2.6 lakh before subsidy. After the PM Surya Ghar central subsidy of ₹78,000, your net cost ranges from approximately ₹1.02–1.82 lakh, depending on panel brand, inverter type, and roof complexity.

What is the payback period for rooftop solar in Hyderabad?

Payback depends on your actual electricity consumption, TSSPDCL tariff slab (₹6.05–₹9.95 per unit for domestic consumers), and system generation. Most Hyderabad homeowners can expect payback within 4–6 years, though results vary based on usage patterns and shadow-free roof area.

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy apply to 5 kW or 10 kW systems?

Yes, but the subsidy is capped at ₹78,000 regardless of system size above 3 kW. Larger systems still benefit from the subsidy, but the absolute amount does not increase beyond the cap.

What warranty should I expect from a Hyderabad solar installer?

Standard warranties include a 25-year performance warranty on panels, a 5–10 year product warranty on inverters, and a minimum 5-year workmanship warranty on installation. Confirm all warranty terms in writing before signing your contract.

How long does TSSPDCL net-metering approval take?

Timelines typically range from two to eight weeks after installation, depending on documentation completeness and division workload. An experienced EPC contractor who manages the application end-to-end can help avoid common delays and rejections.
