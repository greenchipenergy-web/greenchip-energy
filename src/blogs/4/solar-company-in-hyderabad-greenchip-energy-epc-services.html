PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 Subsidy: How Hyderabad Homes Claim It

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, launched in February 2024, remains the single biggest financial incentive driving residential solar adoption across India. Under this central government scheme, domestic consumers in Hyderabad can claim a subsidy of up to ₹78,000 on rooftop solar systems—₹30,000 per kW for the first 3 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the next 7 kW (systems up to 10 kW qualify). The subsidy is credited directly to the homeowner's bank account after DISCOM verification and meter installation.

For a typical 3 kW system in Hyderabad, the subsidy amounts to ₹78,000 (3 × ₹30,000 + ₹18,000 × 0 kW for additional capacity, capped at the ₹78,000 maximum for 3 kW). A 5 kW system receives ₹78,000 for the first 3 kW plus ₹36,000 for the remaining 2 kW, totalling ₹1,14,000 in subsidy support. These are fixed central government rates applicable across Telangana.

The claim process follows these steps:

  1. Register on the national portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) with your electricity consumer number
  2. Get a feasibility check from your local DISCOM (TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL)
  3. Choose an installer and get the system installed
  4. Apply for net metering with your DISCOM
  5. DISCOM inspects and installs the bidirectional meter
  6. Subsidy disbursed to your bank account after commissioning

The critical variable is step 3. Choosing an installer with direct experience navigating solar EPC solutions for Hyderabad's DISCOM requirements can significantly reduce approval delays and paperwork errors.

TSSPDCL Net-Metering Process & Approval Timeline

Net metering is the mechanism that makes rooftop solar financially viable in Hyderabad. When your solar system generates more electricity than your home consumes during the day, the surplus flows into the TSSPDCL grid. Your bidirectional meter runs backward, crediting those units against your nighttime consumption. At month's end, you pay only the net difference—or receive a credit if generation exceeds usage.

The TSERC's draft Rooftop Solar Regulation 2025, issued on 14 August 2025, has modernized the framework significantly. Key provisions include:

  • Net metering available for systems up to 500 kW
  • Group net metering for housing societies sharing a single rooftop
  • Virtual net metering for consumers below 100 kW in shared configurations
  • Gross metering option for systems up to 1 MW
  • Deemed approval for systems up to 10 kW within 15 working days
  • Transformer capacity cap: cumulative rooftop solar limited to 50% of distribution transformer capacity

For a residential homeowner applying for a 3 kW system, the typical net-metering flow involves: (1) submitting application with system details and single-line diagram, (2) DISCOM feasibility check, (3) system installation and inspection, (4) bidirectional meter installation, and (5) commissioning sign-off. With deemed approval provisions now in place, the process for systems under 10 kW is substantially faster than the multi-month timelines previously experienced.

Apartment complexes benefit most from the group and virtual net metering provisions—channels that didn't exist under the 2016 regulation. How to Choose the Best Solar Installer in Hyderabad: 12-Point Checklist

Before signing any contract, run your shortlisted installer through this 12-point vetting checklist. Each point addresses a common source of post-installation disputes, subsidy rejections, or performance disappointment:

  1. DISCOM Empanelment: Confirm TGREDCO or MNRE empanelment—unempanelled installers cannot claim the PM Surya Ghar subsidy on your behalf
  2. ALMM-Listed Panels: Only panels on the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers qualify for subsidy; non-ALMM installations are automatically rejected
  3. GST Invoice: Demand a proper GST invoice—this is mandatory for subsidy claims and warranty activation
  4. Detailed Quotation: The quote should itemise panels, inverter, mounting structure, wiring, installation labour, and commissioning separately
  5. Written Warranty Terms: Get panel warranty (25-year performance + 10–12 year product) and workmanship warranty (5–10 years) documented in the contract
  6. Site Survey Before Quoting: Quality installers conduct a physical site assessment—evaluating roof strength, shadow patterns, orientation, and electrical panel compatibility—before providing a price
  7. Subsidy Application Handling: Verify the installer will complete PM Surya Ghar portal registration and TSSPDCL net-metering paperwork for you
  8. Installation Timeline: Get a committed timeline with milestone dates for delivery, installation, and grid synchronization
  9. O&M Offering: Solar plants need periodic cleaning, inverter checks, and performance monitoring—ask about AMC or O&M service options
  10. Customer References: Request Hyderabad-specific installations you can verify, not just online testimonials
  11. Inverter Warranty: Tier-1 inverters typically carry 5–10 year warranties; microinverters may offer longer coverage
  12. Transparent Pricing Model: Understand whether pricing is per-kW fixed, tiered package-based, or custom post-assessment

Red Flags to Avoid When Hiring a Hyderabad Solar Company

The Hyderabad solar market, while growing, still has its share of inexperienced or unscrupulous operators. Watch for these warning signs during your evaluation:

  • 100% advance demand: Reputable EPC providers collect payment in stages tied to project milestones—design sign-off, material delivery, installation completion, and commissioning. Full upfront payment removes your leverage if the project stalls.
  • Non-ALMM panels: Some installers push cheaper, non-listed panels to reduce the quote. This directly disqualifies your PM Surya Ghar subsidy claim. Always verify the panel model against MNRE's ALMM list before signing.
  • No GST invoice: A GST invoice is not optional—it's required for both subsidy processing and warranty claims. Installers who offer "cheaper rates without GST" are exposing you to subsidy rejection.
  • Guaranteed savings promises: No installer can promise specific unit generation, exact payback years, or guaranteed bill reduction. Actual output depends on your consumption pattern, TSSPDCL tariff slab, roof orientation, and seasonal variation.
  • No site survey: Quoting without a physical roof assessment—checking structural load capacity, shadow analysis, and electrical compatibility—is a recipe for underperformance.
  • Vague warranty language: "25-year warranty" means different things to different companies. Ensure the contract specifies performance warranty terms, product warranty duration, and workmanship warranty scope in writing.
  • No O&M plan: Solar systems require routine cleaning and periodic technical checks. Installers who disappear after commissioning leave you without support for a 25-year asset.

Hyderabad Solar FAQs: Cost, Subsidy, Payback & Warranty

What is a solar company in Hyderabad?

A solar company in Hyderabad provides end-to-end rooftop or ground-mount solar power solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial customers. Services typically include system design, component procurement, installation, TSSPDCL net-metering coordination, PM Surya Ghar subsidy assistance, and post-installation maintenance.

What is the cost of a 3 kW solar system in Hyderabad after subsidy?

A 3 kW rooftop solar system in Hyderabad typically costs ₹1.8–2.4 lakh before subsidy. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, the net cost ranges from approximately ₹1.02–1.62 lakh, depending on panel brand, inverter type, and installation complexity.

Does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy cover 100% of installation costs?

No. The central government subsidy covers a significant portion—up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW systems and up to ₹1,80,000 for 10 kW systems—but homeowners must fund the remaining balance. State-level incentives and accelerated depreciation benefits for commercial properties may provide additional financial support.

How long does TSSPDCL net-metering approval take in Hyderabad?

Under the TSERC's 2025 draft regulation, systems up to 10 kW qualify for deemed approval within 15 working days after DISCOM inspection. Larger systems and applications in areas with saturated transformer capacity may take longer. Timelines vary case-by-case.

What happens during cloudy or rainy days—does solar still work?

Solar panels generate reduced but non-zero output during overcast conditions. Hyderabad's predominantly sunny climate (300+ sunny days annually) means cloudy-day losses are minimal over a full year. Net metering credits accumulated during high-generation months offset lower-generation periods.

Can apartment residents in Hyderabad install rooftop solar?

Yes. The TSERC's 2025 regulation introduces group net metering and virtual net metering provisions specifically for housing societies and apartment complexes. These models allow multiple residents to share a common rooftop installation and receive individual billing credits—an option that didn't exist under the previous 2016 framework.

What is the payback period for residential solar in Hyderabad?

Can Hyderabad apartment complexes get rooftop solar with individual billing?

Yes. The TSERC's 2025 draft regulation introduces group net metering and virtual net metering, allowing apartment residents to share a common rooftop solar system and receive individual billing credits through their respective TSSPDCL meters.

What documents are needed for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy in Hyderabad?

You'll need your TSSPDCL/TSNPDCL electricity consumer number, Aadhaar-linked bank account, GST invoice from the installer, system commissioning report, and net-metering completion certificate. The installer typically assists with portal registration and document submission.

Are non-ALMM solar panels eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

No. Only panels listed on MNRE's Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) qualify for the central subsidy. Installing non-ALMM panels will result in automatic subsidy rejection by the DISCOM during verification.
