About GreenChip Energy

GreenChip Energy — the brand operating under GreenChip Emerging Projects Private Limited — is a solar EPC service provider headquartered in Hyderabad, India. The company positions itself under the tagline "Smart Solar EPC Solutions in India," covering the full project lifecycle from engineering design through procurement, construction, and commissioning. For decision-makers evaluating solar vendors in a competitive market, GreenChip's turnkey approach offers a meaningful advantage: a single provider managing every phase of a solar project, rather than fragmenting responsibility across multiple contractors or sub-vendors. This model is particularly relevant for clients seeking accountability, streamlined communication, and faster project timelines.

India's solar landscape provides the context for GreenChip's relevance. According to the Press Information Bureau (Government of India), the country's installed solar capacity crossed 129 GW in 2025 — a more than 40-fold increase from just 3 GW in 2014. Non-fossil power sources now account for over 50% of India's 500 GW total installed capacity, and more than 55 solar parks with a combined sanctioned capacity of 40 GW have been approved across 13 states. This explosive capacity build-out has created robust, sustained demand for EPC companies capable of executing projects at every scale — from a homeowner's rooftop system to multi-megawatt industrial installations. GreenChip Energy serves this full spectrum, positioning its turnkey capabilities for residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale solar project delivery across India.

Our Core Business Activities

GreenChip Energy's core business revolves around solar EPC — Engineering, Procurement, and Construction — delivered across all major market segments in India. On the residential side, the company provides rooftop solar EPC for homeowners, managing everything from system design and layout to procurement, installation, and grid-connected commissioning. For commercial and industrial (C&I) clients, GreenChip handles rooftop and ground-mount solar systems for factories, warehouses, and large commercial facilities. The company also executes utility-scale projects for developers and investors requiring high-capacity delivery. Beyond standard solar, GreenChip's solution portfolio extends to Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), floating solar, solar carports, hybrid solar-wind systems, and sub-station infrastructure — reflecting multi-technology expertise that goes well beyond basic rooftop EPC.

The company's service model also includes Operations and Maintenance (O&M) for existing solar plants, Group Captive solar projects, SCADA-based monitoring, and asset management. This breadth matters in India's fast-growing market. According to Mercom India Research, India installed a record 7.1 GW of rooftop solar capacity in 2025 alone — a 122% jump from 3.2 GW in 2024 — with the residential sector driving nearly 76% of additions under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana scheme. Industrial and commercial segments contributed 18% and 5% of additions respectively. As more homeowners, factories, and developers adopt solar, the demand for experienced Smart Solar EPC Solutions in India with multi-technology capabilities continues to rise.

Company Details

GreenChip Emerging Projects Private Limited is a privately held company incorporated in India. Based on publicly available corporate and professional profile information, the company was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana. With a team size estimated between 11 and 50 employees, GreenChip operates as a lean but specialised solar EPC firm — a structure common among India's growing cohort of focused renewable energy service providers. The company's registered name reflects broader infrastructure ambitions, while its public-facing brand, GreenChip Energy, is tightly centred on solar EPC delivery under the tagline "Smart Solar EPC Solutions in India."

The business serves clients across India, spanning residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale segments. GreenChip's engagement models include direct EPC procurement — where the client owns the solar system outright — and PPA/OPEX (Power Purchase Agreement / Operational Expenditure) models, where the solar infrastructure is deployed at zero upfront capital cost and the client pays for the electricity generated over a contract period. This dual-model approach enables GreenChip to serve cost-conscious homeowners seeking affordable turnkey rooftop delivery as well as C&I facility owners who prefer capex-free solar adoption. Pricing is customised per project and typically determined after a site assessment, which serves as the company's primary initial engagement step for evaluating feasibility, system sizing, and site-specific requirements before a tailored proposal is presented.

Expert Insights: India's Solar EPC Outlook

The solar EPC market in India is entering a period of sustained, large-scale growth. CRISIL MI&A Consulting projects 180–185 GW of solar capacity additions between FY2025 and FY2029, more than triple the 50–55 GW added in the preceding five-year period. Rooftop solar alone is expected to contribute 20–22 GW by FY29, with the PM Surya Ghar scheme continuing to drive residential adoption. Third-party EPC service providers are estimated to capture 40–45% of this market, making experienced EPC firms central to India's solar execution pipeline. The typical solar EPC cost in India currently ranges between ₹45,000–50,000 per kW, though actual pricing varies by project scale, technology choices, and site conditions.

Looking at technology trends, the integration of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) alongside solar is accelerating, driven by grid stability requirements and time-of-day tariff structures. Hybrid solar-wind projects, floating solar on reservoirs and industrial water bodies, and solar carport solutions for commercial parking areas represent growing niche segments. Companies like GreenChip Energy, which list BESS, floating solar, hybrid, and carport among their solution offerings, are positioning for these emerging opportunities well before they become mainstream market requirements. For project developers, C&I facility owners, and homeowners, the key differentiator in selecting an EPC partner is shifting from price alone to demonstrated multi-technology execution capability, engineering depth, and the ability to deliver reliable O&M support over a plant's 25-year operational life. The EPC partner you choose today will determine not just your installation quality, but your long-term asset performance.
