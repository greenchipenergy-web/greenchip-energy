PM-DHARA Scheme 2026: ₹1.86 Lakh Crore Green Energy Corridor, 135 GW Renewable Energy & 50 GWh BESS

India is entering a major new phase of renewable energy development with the approval of Green Energy Corridor Phase-III (GEC-III), a large-scale initiative to strengthen intra-state transmission infrastructure and accelerate the integration of renewable energy into India's power grid.

The programme has a total outlay of approximately ₹1.86 lakh crore and is designed to support the evacuation and integration of up to 135 GW of renewable energy, together with 50 GWh of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

For solar developers, EPC companies, commercial and industrial consumers, BESS developers and renewable energy investors, this development creates significant opportunities across India's renewable energy value chain.

What is PM-DHARA / Green Energy Corridor Phase-III?

The Government of India has approved Green Energy Corridor Phase-III (GEC-III) to strengthen the Intra-State Transmission System (InSTS) and facilitate the evacuation of renewable power.

The programme is targeted for implementation by FY 2032-33.

According to the Government of India's announcement, the programme has a total project outlay of ₹1,86,405 crore.

The major components include:

ComponentAllocation
Intra-State Transmission System₹1,36,378 crore
Battery Energy Storage Systems₹50,000 crore
Total Project Outlay₹1,86,405 crore
Central Financial Support₹54,082 crore
Renewable Energy EvacuationUp to 135 GW
Battery Energy Storage50 GWh

The objective is to strengthen the transmission network and facilitate the integration of renewable energy while addressing challenges such as renewable intermittency, transmission congestion and peak-hour curtailment.

For companies such as Greenchip Energy, this expansion is relevant because future solar projects will increasingly need to be designed together with grid connectivity, evacuation infrastructure and energy storage.

50 GWh Battery Energy Storage – Why BESS Matters

Another major component of the programme is 50 GWh of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

Battery storage can help address the mismatch between renewable generation and electricity consumption.

For example, a solar plant generates electricity primarily during daylight hours, while a commercial or industrial consumer may have substantial electricity demand during evening or peak hours.

A BESS can store electricity during periods of renewable generation and discharge it when required.

Solar + BESS can potentially provide:

  • Peak-hour power
  • Energy shifting
  • Renewable power during non-solar hours
  • Improved renewable utilisation
  • Grid balancing
  • Reduced curtailment
  • Better demand management
  • Support for firm renewable power

This creates a major opportunity for Solar + BESS project development.

Greenchip Energy can evaluate the customer's electricity consumption profile and determine whether a combination of solar generation and battery storage could improve the technical and commercial performance of the project.

Frequently Asked Questions About PM-DHARA

What is PM-DHARA?

The Government's current Cabinet-approved programme is officially described as Green Energy Corridor Phase-III (GEC-III). It focuses on strengthening intra-state transmission infrastructure and integrating renewable energy.

What is the total investment under GEC-III?

The total approved project outlay is approximately ₹1,86,405 crore.

How much is allocated for transmission?

Approximately ₹1,36,378 crore is allocated for intra-state transmission infrastructure.

How much is allocated for BESS?

Approximately ₹50,000 crore is allocated for Battery Energy Storage Systems, targeting 50 GWh of storage.

How much renewable energy can the scheme facilitate?

The programme is intended to facilitate evacuation and integration of up to 135 GW of renewable energy.

Is ₹1.86 lakh crore a direct subsidy for solar companies?

No.

The ₹1.86 lakh crore represents the overall programme outlay. It should not be interpreted as a direct subsidy available to solar EPC companies or individual solar developers.

What is BESS?

BESS means Battery Energy Storage System.

It stores electrical energy and discharges it when electricity is required.

Why is BESS important for solar?

Solar generation occurs mainly during daylight hours. BESS can help shift renewable electricity to periods when solar generation is lower and electricity demand may be higher.
