Core Business Activities: Solar EPC and Beyond

At its foundation, GreenChip Energy delivers solar EPC — a turnkey project delivery model encompassing three integrated phases. The Engineering phase covers site-specific system design, electrical layout planning, structural assessment, and shading analysis tailored to each installation. The Procurement phase involves sourcing solar panels, inverters, mounting structures, and balance-of-system (BOS) components from qualified suppliers. The Construction phase brings the project to life through on-site installation, electrical wiring, testing, and grid integration, culminating in commissioning and performance validation before handover. This three-phase model ensures a single point of accountability from concept to completion — a structure that eliminates coordination gaps between separate design, supply, and installation vendors.

The company's service scope spans all major solar segments. For homeowners, GreenChip Energy offers affordable turnkey residential rooftop solar EPC, covering design through commissioning as a structured path to reducing monthly electricity bills. For commercial and industrial clients, the company provides hassle-free C&I solar EPC for factories, warehouses, and large rooftop or ground-mount systems — with the added option of capex-free Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) and OPEX subscription models that eliminate upfront capital requirements. India's residential solar demand is meanwhile surging: the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana crossed 10 lakh solar-powered homes by March 2025, with 47.3 lakh applications received and ₹4,770 crore in subsidies disbursed, according to the Press Information Bureau. The scheme targets 1 crore installations by March 2027, backed by ₹75,000 crore in government funding. Beyond core EPC delivery, GreenChip Energy also offers Operations and Maintenance (O&M) services for existing solar plants — a critical service line for asset owners seeking long-term performance assurance beyond initial commissioning.

Expert Insights: The Future of Smart Solar EPC in India

The solar EPC industry in India is undergoing a fundamental transformation. The era of EPC as a pure construction exercise — mount panels, connect wires, synchronize with the grid — is giving way to a more integrated, technology-driven delivery model. Digital twin technology, which creates a real-time virtual replica of solar assets fed by IoT sensor data, has emerged as a key operational tool. According to industry analysis, large-scale operators have reported reductions in unplanned downtime of up to 25% through predictive maintenance enabled by digital twins, with developers in Rajasthan and Gujarat increasingly integrating digital shadow technology into their O&M strategies. For EPC providers, embedding digital monitoring and predictive analytics capabilities from the point of commissioning is becoming a competitive differentiator rather than an optional add-on.

Looking ahead, India's solar EPC sector will be shaped by three converging forces: the growth of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), the rise of hybrid solar-wind configurations, and the expansion of floating solar installations. EPC providers who can offer integrated solutions across these technologies — rather than siloed panel installations — will capture the next wave of demand. GreenChip Energy's referenced capabilities in BESS and wind energy suggest an awareness of this multi-technology direction. As India targets 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030, the EPC companies that combine engineering depth, procurement scale, digital tools, and multi-technology integration will define the industry's next chapter. The smart EPC provider of 2026 is not just a builder — it is a technology-enabled energy infrastructure partner.
