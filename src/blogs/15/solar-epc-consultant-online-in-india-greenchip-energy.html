What Is a Solar EPC Company and How Does It Work in India

EPC stands for Engineering, Procurement, and Construction — a turnkey project delivery model where a single provider manages the complete lifecycle of a solar power plant. Instead of hiring separate designers, sourcing equipment from multiple vendors, and coordinating independent installation crews, the buyer works with one entity accountable from feasibility study through grid synchronization. This consolidation reduces coordination risk, shortens timelines, and creates a single point of contact for warranty and performance-related claims. GreenChip Energy solar EPC solutions operate on this same turnkey principle, covering residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale projects across India.

In India, solar EPC companies typically serve three segments: residential rooftop (1–10 kW for homeowners), commercial and industrial or C&I (50 kW–10 MW for factories, warehouses, and office campuses), and utility-scale (10 MW+ for ground-mounted solar parks). A standard EPC scope includes site assessment, system design, procurement of ALMM-listed solar modules and inverters, civil and electrical installation, DISCOM liaison for net-metering approvals, and commissioning. Operations & Maintenance (O&M) often follows as a separate engagement covering panel cleaning, inverter diagnostics, and generation monitoring — essential for maintaining plant output across a 25-year asset life.

Solar EPC Delivery Phases — Engineering, Procurement & Construction

Understanding what happens inside an EPC engagement helps you ask the right questions when evaluating proposals. Every solar EPC project follows three core phases regardless of scale.

Engineering encompasses site survey (physical or satellite-based for online consultations), shadow analysis, electrical design, structural load assessment for rooftop projects, single-line diagrams (SLDs), and a detailed Bill of Materials (BOM). For rooftop projects, this phase also includes preparing DISCOM applications for net-metering approvals. Procurement follows — sourcing ALMM-approved solar modules, selecting inverters matched to system size and grid specifications, and acquiring mounting structures, cables, connectors, junction boxes, and all balance-of-system (BOS) components. Providers with established sourcing relationships can often deliver better equipment lead times and warranty stacking. Construction covers on-site execution: civil works for ground-mount systems, structural mounting on rooftops, module and inverter installation, DC and AC cabling, earthing, and grid synchronization. GreenChip Energy covers all three phases as a turnkey provider. The final step — commissioning — involves inverter energization, meter installation, DISCOM inspection, and performance validation against design benchmarks before handover.

Solar EPC Cost Benchmarks 2026 — What to Budget by Segment

Cost transparency remains one of the biggest gaps in India's solar EPC market — and it's precisely where online consultants add value by sharing indicative price ranges before any site visit.

For residential rooftop systems (1–10 kW), EPC costs generally range from ₹35,000–₹55,000 per kW pre-subsidy, depending on module tier, inverter brand, mounting type, and state-level DISCOM charges. After PM Surya Ghar subsidies, net costs for a 3 kW system can fall to ₹15,000–₹45,000 depending on capacity slab and location. C&I projects (50 kW–10 MW) typically range from ₹30,000–₹48,000 per kW, with scale-based procurement advantages. Many C&I buyers prefer zero-capex PPA/OPEX models where they pay per unit of electricity rather than owning the system. Utility-scale EPC (10 MW+) ranges from ₹28,000–₹38,000 per kW, benefiting from economies of scale in module procurement and workforce deployment. These are indicative ranges — actual pricing varies with site conditions, module technology (mono-PERC vs. TOPCon vs. HJT), inverter specifications, and grid-compliance requirements. A credible solar EPC consultant online will provide itemized cost breakups after a site assessment. GreenChip Energy follows this model, offering custom quotes after assessing project specifics. Always request separate line items for equipment, installation, DISCOM fees, and commissioning.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy, Net-Metering & Online EPC Guidance

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana has fundamentally transformed residential solar economics. As of December 2025, the scheme powered 23.96 lakh households with rooftop solar installations, with ₹13,464.6 crore in subsidies disbursed directly to beneficiary bank accounts through the National Portal, according to the Press Information Bureau. The scheme targets 1 crore households by FY 2026-27 and offers collateral-free loans at 6% per annum for 10 years, supporting both CAPEX and RESCO/Utility Led Aggregation models.

For online EPC selection, the critical detail is this: the entire PM Surya Ghar journey — registration, vendor selection, installation documentation, and subsidy credit — runs through a digital portal. An experienced online EPC consultant can guide homeowners through the process remotely: registering on the portal, coordinating the site assessment, managing post-installation documentation, and tracking subsidy disbursement. However, buyers should verify that their chosen EPC is a registered vendor with the local DISCOM. No single EPC is empanelled in every state — DISCOM vendor lists vary by region. The right solar EPC consultant in India will be transparent about which DISCOMs they're registered with and handle the full subsidy-to-synchronization workflow as part of their turnkey engagement.

How to Evaluate a Solar EPC Consultant Online — Buyer Scorecard

Before engaging any EPC consultant found online, run them through this 10-parameter evaluation framework to separate credible providers from surface-level marketing.

Engineering capability (20 points): Does the consultant provide detailed system designs — SLDs, shadow analysis, structural load reports — or just generic proposals? In-house engineering is a strong quality signal. Equipment sourcing transparency (15 points): Will they specify module brand, model, wattage, inverter make, and warranty terms in writing? Vague sourcing language is a red flag. DISCOM coordination experience (15 points): Can they handle net-metering applications, CEI inspections, and subsidy paperwork in your specific state? O&M commitment (10 points): Do they offer post-installation maintenance with defined service-level terms? PPA/OPEX availability (10 points): For C&I buyers, does the consultant offer capex-free engagement models? Cost transparency (10 points): Is the proposal itemized by equipment, installation, and commissioning — or a single lump sum? Reference availability (10 points): Will they share contacts or site details for completed projects in your segment? Financial strength (5 points): Can they provide bank guarantees or EPC bonds on larger projects? ALMM compliance (5 points): Do they source exclusively from ALMM-listed module manufacturers? When evaluating any online solar EPC consultant, request written responses across each criterion before signing an engagement letter.

What is a solar EPC consultant online?

A solar EPC consultant online is a service provider who offers end-to-end solar project delivery — engineering design, equipment procurement, construction, and commissioning — through digital-first consultations, virtual site assessments, and remote project management, eliminating the need for in-person visits during early project stages.

How much does solar EPC cost per kW in India in 2026?

Residential rooftop EPC costs range from ₹35,000–₹55,000 per kW before subsidies, C&I projects range from ₹30,000–₹48,000 per kW, and utility-scale EPC ranges from ₹28,000–₹38,000 per kW. Actual costs depend on module technology, inverter brand, site conditions, and DISCOM requirements.

Can I get a PM Surya Ghar subsidy through an online EPC consultant?

Yes. The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana operates entirely through a digital National Portal. An experienced online EPC consultant can register your application, coordinate the site assessment, manage installation documentation, and track subsidy disbursement — all remotely. What should I check before hiring a solar EPC consultant online?

Verify engineering capability (detailed designs, not generic proposals), equipment sourcing transparency (specific brands and model numbers in writing), DISCOM coordination experience in your state, O&M support terms, cost proposal itemization, and availability of references from completed projects in your segment.

Is PPA/OPEX solar available for commercial and industrial buyers?

Yes. Many EPC consultants offer Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) and OPEX-based models for C&I clients, where the EPC provider owns and operates the solar system while the buyer pays per unit of electricity consumed — eliminating upfront capital expenditure entirely.

What is ALMM compliance in solar EPC procurement?

ALMM stands for the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers maintained by MNRE. Compliant solar EPC consultants source modules exclusively from ALMM-listed manufacturers, which is a mandatory requirement for projects claiming PM Surya Ghar subsidies or government incentives.

Which states have the best solar EPC consultant availability?

States with the highest installed solar capacity — including Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka — typically have the most EPC consultants and DISCOM-approved vendors. However, online EPC consultations allow buyers in any state to access nationally capable providers.

How long does a residential rooftop solar EPC project take from consultation to commissioning?

A typical residential rooftop EPC project takes 4–8 weeks from initial consultation to commissioning, depending on DISCOM approval timelines, equipment availability, and site-specific conditions. Online consultations can compress the early stages (assessment and proposal) to 3–5 working days.
