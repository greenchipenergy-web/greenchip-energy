About

GreenChip Emerging Projects Private Limited, operating under the brand name GreenChip Energy, is a solar EPC service provider headquartered in Hyderabad, India. The company specialises in delivering turnkey solar energy solutions that cover the full project lifecycle — from initial engineering design and component procurement through to on-site construction and commissioning. Positioned as a provider of GreenChip Energy – Smart Solar EPC Solutions in India, the company targets homeowners, commercial and industrial (C&I) facility owners, and project developers seeking reliable renewable energy infrastructure.

The timing for a solar EPC company in India could not be more promising. According to JMK Research's Annual India Solar Report Card, India installed a record 36.5 GW of solar capacity in calendar year 2025, with the rooftop segment alone touching 7.9 GW — 6,835 MW under the capex model and 1,113 MW under the opex model (pv magazine India, April 2026). The country is projected to commission approximately 32.5 GW of new utility-scale solar and 8.5 GW of rooftop solar in 2026. For integrated EPC providers like GreenChip Energy, this growth trajectory represents a substantial multi-year opportunity across every solar segment — residential, C&I, and utility-scale alike.

Our Core Business Activities

GreenChip Energy's core service offering revolves around solar EPC — a turnkey project delivery model in which a single provider handles Engineering (system design, layout, and technical planning), Procurement (sourcing of solar panels, inverters, mounting structures, and balance-of-system components), and Construction (on-site installation, electrical work, grid integration, and commissioning). According to the GreenChip Energy official website, the company's solution portfolio spans multiple solar formats: rooftop, ground-mounted, battery energy storage systems (BESS), floating solar, solar carport, hybrid solar-wind, and sub-station projects. This breadth of capability allows the company to serve diverse site conditions and project requirements across India.

Beyond EPC delivery, GreenChip Energy also provides Operations and Maintenance (O&M) services for existing solar plants, supporting long-term plant performance and reliability. The company offers capex-free Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) and OPEX subscription-based solar models — engagement structures particularly relevant for C&I facility owners who prefer to pay for solar electricity generated rather than own the system outright. This combination of capex and opex engagement models, paired with multi-format EPC execution, positions GreenChip Energy as a versatile partner for projects ranging from a homeowner's rooftop installation to an industrial ground-mount system.

Our Mission & Vision

GreenChip Energy operates at the intersection of two macro themes reshaping India's energy landscape: industry decarbonisation and the digitalisation of energy infrastructure. The company's stated mission centres on decarbonising industrial infrastructure while simultaneously digitalising and decentralising energy solutions — a dual objective that reflects the evolving expectations of C&I buyers, developers, and public-sector stakeholders seeking not just kilowatt-hours, but intelligent, data-connected energy systems.

India's broader policy environment reinforces this vision. According to the Press Information Bureau, India's total installed solar capacity reached 129 GW in 2025, up from just 3 GW in 2014 — a roughly 43-fold increase in barely a decade. Non-fossil energy sources now account for over 50% of India's 500 GW total power capacity. Additionally, 55 solar parks with a combined sanctioned capacity of 40 GW have been approved across 13 Indian states (PIB, October 2025). For GreenChip Energy, this policy momentum validates the company's strategic focus on building the infrastructure backbone that enables India's transition to clean, decentralised energy.

Expert Insights

India's solar EPC landscape is entering a phase of accelerated growth and specialisation. The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana crossed 9.56 GW of rooftop solar capacity by March 2026, with 23.9 lakh households adopting rooftop solar and ₹13,464.6 crore in subsidies released by December 2025 (Press Information Bureau). The scheme targets 1 crore households with a ₹75,021 crore outlay and is expected to add 30 GW of residential rooftop solar capacity — a pipeline that expands GreenChip Energy's addressable market significantly.

Several trends will shape how companies like GreenChip Energy compete going forward. First, the convergence of solar with battery energy storage (BESS) and hybrid renewable configurations is creating demand for EPC providers with multi-technology integration capabilities. Second, the rise of capex-free PPA and OPEX models is democratising solar adoption among C&I clients who prefer operational expenditure over capital investment. Third, digitalisation of energy assets — from SCADA monitoring to AI-driven performance optimisation — is becoming a differentiator for EPC firms offering intelligent energy management. For decision-makers evaluating EPC partners in 2026, the question is no longer just "who can install panels?" but "who can deliver a fully integrated energy system with long-term operational support?"
